## How I think about AI products

1. **Start with the decision.** Define the decision the product changes and the business metric it moves before picking a model.
2. **Evaluate the outcome, not the wording.** Airboard's evals check the final board state; Vibe's check the recommendation, not the prose around it.
3. **Bound the model.** Rules, typed plans and guardrails make AI predictable enough for enterprises to trust.
